RadioPocket 0.8
* Changed: The Station Library is one continuous list instead of pages — more stations load automatically as you scroll, and Previous/Next are gone
* Added: A real Fire visualization — a flame simulation fed by the spectrum — in the visualization window, full screen, and as the player's Fire mini mode
* Added: Visualization Settings (Visualization menu or Preferences): peak falloff, band line width, flame height, turbulence and colors (Flame, Blue Flame, Toxic Green, Skin Colors), plus the existing options in one place
* Added: Radio Paradise direct AAC mixes, Mike Pierce’s Curated Internet Radio Streams, and optional Junguler Radio Playlists bring the built-in source count to eight
* Improved: Playlist genre comments and named collection sections are searchable; curated archive loading excludes DEMOTED files and uses one bounded request
* Changed: Clicking the player's visualizer cycles Bars / Fire / Oscilloscope; double-click opens the visualization window. The window's coloring picker is gone (K and the Visualization menu still cycle coloring)
* Changed: The player now has Winamp's EQ and PL buttons (PL opens the Station Library); + FAV, LIB and SK moved to the ≡ menu and keep their shortcuts (D, B, K)
* Improved: The visualization window can be resized down to the player's width
* Added: Jump to Station (J, or Shift-Cmd-J): type part of a name, arrows to choose, Return to tune in; searches favorites and recent stations
* Added: Station Info (I, or Shift-Cmd-I): live format, sample rate, measured bitrate, rewind buffer, leveler gain, and an on-request Radio Browser lookup for country, genre and homepage
* Added: The player shows the stream's real kbps and kHz and the mono/stereo lamp (skins that include MONOSTER.BMP)
* Added: Sleep Timer (Radio menu): 15 minutes to 2 hours, with a 20-second volume fade; the countdown shows on the display
* Added: Loudness Leveling (Radio menu): evens out loud and quiet stations with a slow gain follower and a soft limiter; off by default
* Added: Windows snap to screen edges and to each other, and the equalizer docked under the player moves with it; hold Option to drag freely
* Improved: Double-click the player or equalizer title bar to window-shade it; a docked equalizer follows the shaded player
* Added: Nine more visualizations for the window and full screen: Waveform Ring, Vectorscope, Waterfall, Starfield, Spectrum Tunnel, Plasma, Kaleidoscope, Fireworks and Aurora; the beat-driven ones react to the bass, and all use the skin's visualizer colors. Keys 1–9 and 0 jump to the first ten
* Added: Five built-in directory sources: Radio Browser, SomaFM, laut.fm, Icecast / Xiph and IPRD; full-catalog feeds are opt-in to keep ordinary searches small
* Improved: Directory settings scroll to fit built-in and custom sources, preserving saved enable/disable choices
* Fixed: M3U names parse correctly when quoted metadata contains commas
* Added: More station directories: SomaFM is built in, and Preferences > Directories turns directories on or off and adds your own (a Radio Browser-compatible server, or a link to an M3U / PLS station list)
* Added: A directory picker in the library window searches every enabled directory together, or one at a time
* Fixed: The library window can be closed and minimized from its title bar; its resize edges no longer cover the buttons
* Fixed: Skinned players no longer paint black boxes over the skin, and the play indicator and + FAV / LIB buttons follow Winamp's layout
* Added: Ogg Opus radio streams play, using the Opus decoder built into macOS (no bundled codec); stream titles come from the Opus tags
* Added: HLS streams play through RadioPocket's own HLS client: master playlists, live playlists, MPEG-TS and packed AAC/MP3 segments, AES-128 encryption and ID3 titles
* Improved: HLS and Opus now have the equalizer and the visualizer, because every stream goes through the same pipeline
* Added: Pause keeps the connection and buffers up to five minutes, so Play continues where you stopped; Left/Right (or [ and ]) rewind and forward 10 seconds and L (or Cmd-J) jumps back to live
* Added: Media-key and Control Center skip buttons rewind and forward 10 seconds
* Improved: Dropped connections reconnect automatically with backoff instead of giving up after three tries, and a stream that goes silent without closing is restarted after 20 seconds
* Improved: Playback reconnects when the network returns or changes (Wi-Fi to Ethernet) and after the Mac wakes from sleep
* Added: When a station can't be reached, RadioPocket asks the directory whether it moved and retries the new address, then offers the closest alternative, Find Similar, or the next favorite
* Added: Opus filter in the library; Opus and Ogg stations are listed as playable
* Fixed: The display line shows reconnecting, paused and delayed status instead of only failures
* Removed: The old 8-buffer network backpressure; a slow output can no longer stall the connection
* Added: Ogg Vorbis streams play (stb_vorbis, MIT/public domain, bundled unmodified in ThirdParty/stb); chained streams and title changes are followed
* Added: Ogg Vorbis filter in the library
* Misc: AVPlayer remains only as a fallback for fragmented-MP4 and SAMPLE-AES HLS; those streams have no EQ or visualizer
* Misc: test-streams.sh runs the app against local Opus, HLS (TS, packed, AES, master), ICY, reconnect and time-shift fixtures

RadioPocket 0.7
* Added: Native Preferences window with category sidebar, installed skin list and player preview
* Added: Imported skins stay installed; playback, appearance and equalizer preferences are accessible with Command-comma
* Improved: Always-on-top preference survives relaunch
* Improved: Main display follows the original 275 x 116 Winamp coordinate grid
* Improved: Station name now occupies the former seek-bar area, leaving kbps/kHz labels intact
* Improved: Bitmap text is clipped to whole character cells with an ellipsis for overflow
* Improved: Display colors come from the selected skin
* Fixed: Missing audio metrics are shown as dashes instead of guessed values
* Fixed: Volume readout stays inside the former balance-control rectangle
* Fixed: Library headers and custom button labels use integer-aligned baselines
* Added: Version history window (RadioPocket > About RadioPocket) backed by Resources/CHANGELOG.txt
* Added: Real spectrum analyzer and oscilloscope in the player's visualizer slot, driven by the decoded audio (FFT, peak markers, falloff)
* Added: Click the player visualizer to cycle Spectrum / Oscilloscope / Off; right-click for coloring, peaks, falloff, scope style and refresh rate
* Added: Visualization window (Shift-Cmd-V) with Spectrum Bars, Oscilloscope, Mirror, Circular and Spectrogram modes
* Added: Full-screen visualization (double-click the player visualizer or the window, Ctrl-Cmd-F, or F) with Esc to leave, title overlay and an on-screen key list (H)
* Added: Winamp transport hotkeys Z X C V B in the player and visualizer, plus visualizer hotkeys for mode, coloring, peaks, falloff and auto-cycle
* Added: Visualizer colors follow the skin's VISCOLOR.TXT
* Misc: Visualizer timer runs only while a visualizer is on screen and audio is playing, then stops when the bars have fallen
* Misc: Visualizer and EQ are not available for HLS streams: macOS does not give AVPlayer audio to a processing tap, so those streams show their status instead
* Added: Mac media keys (Play/Pause, Next, Previous), AirPods and headset controls now control RadioPocket
* Added: Station and current song title appear in Control Center and the Now Playing menu-bar widget
* Misc: Version number in the app bundle is now read from the top of this file
